Papersun

Paper-sky arcade hopper

Google PlayGalaxy StoreXiaomi GetApps

Tap once. Leap to the next orbit. Don't touch the arcs.

Papersun is a quick, calming one-tap arcade game set in a sky made of cut paper. Your little star circles a glowing sun, and every tap sends it hopping out to the next ring. Time each leap through the gaps between the turning arcs, collect lights on the way and reach the golden ring to clear the level.

Why you'll keep playing

  • One-tap controls you learn in seconds
  • Level after level of slowly rising challenge
  • A new chapter every 20 levels: Dawn, Dunes, Dusk, Night, Bloom and Snowfall, each with its own colors, weather and music
  • A boss level at the end of every chapter
  • Power-ups: shield, magnet, slow motion and ghost
  • Chain close calls to trigger Fever
  • Up to three stars on every level
  • Soft generative music and satisfying haptics

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
