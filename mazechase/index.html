Eat every berry. Dodge the crows. Grab a chili and roll!

Maze Chase: Hedgehog Dash is a classic maze-chase arcade game painted like a storybook. Bramble, a round little hedgehog, trundles through garden hedge mazes gobbling berries while four mischievous crows hop after him. Swipe to steer, and turns you swipe early wait until the path opens. Eat a red chili and Bramble curls into a spiky rolling ball for a few seconds: bowl the crows over and watch them tumble back to their nest in a puff of feathers.

Why you'll keep playing

  • Smooth swipe controls with turn buffering, made for one thumb
  • Four crows with their own personalities: one chases, one cuts you off, one teams up to trap you and one wanders until you come close
  • Chain crow bowls for bigger and bigger combos
  • Bonus acorns, mushrooms and honey jars to snatch
  • Burrows on the sides, one-way hedges and swinging garden gates in later chapters
  • Level after level of slowly rising challenge, with a boss at the end of every chapter: the Magpie, who swoops down from the sky
  • A new garden every 20 levels: Orchard, Wildflower Meadow, Autumn Hedgerow, Snowy Garden, Lantern Night and Mushroom Wood
  • Endless garden mode with a best score to beat
  • Choose your crow pace: Relaxed, Normal or Wild
  • Every berry plays a note of a little melody, and the hedges burst into bloom when you clear a garden
  • Up to three stars on every level

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
