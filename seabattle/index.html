Sea Battle

Classic naval duel, doodled

Google PlayGalaxy StoreXiaomi GetApps

Hide your ships. Call your shots. Sink the whole enemy fleet!

Sea Battle is the classic pencil-and-paper naval duel, drawn in ballpoint pen on a squared school notebook. Drag your cute doodled ships into place, then take turns firing at the enemy sea. A hit lets you fire again. Watch your pen scribble every shot, splash every miss and cross out every ship you sink, before the enemy admiral sinks yours.

What's inside

  • The classic rules on a 10×10 sea, easy to learn in seconds
  • Drag to move, tap to turn, or shuffle your fleet in one tap
  • Five admirals to outwit, from a rookie who fires at random to an admiral who calculates the odds and learns where you like to hide
  • Sonar and Salvo specials charged by your hits
  • A new notebook every 20 levels: Blue Ballpoint, Red Pencil, Green Ink, Kraft Paper, Blueprint and Chalkboard
  • A boss admiral at the end of every chapter, each with a twist: hidden buoys, twin cannons, a wider sea, an armored flagship, a diving submarine and a full armada
  • Quick battle: pick any admiral and your own rules
  • A logbook with your win rate against every admiral
  • Scribbly pen sounds, splashes, booms and up to three stars for sharp shooting

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
