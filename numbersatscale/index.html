Huge numbers you can actually picture.

How tall is a billion dollars stacked in $100 bills? How long would it take to earn a trillion at $1 a second? How old is the light from the nearest star?

Numbers at Scale turns mind-bending money, space and time facts into short, hand-drawn videos narrated by a stick figure with a tape measure. Every number is calculated or sourced, and the math is in each video's description so you can check it yourself.
