Find the move. Deliver the mate. Beat the moonlit bots.

Chess: Moonlit Puzzles & Bots is a calm, beautiful chess trainer set on a marble terrace under a big soft moon. Every level is a real chess puzzle: spot the checkmate, win material with a fork, pin or skewer, or finish an endgame against the engine before your moves run out. Puzzles start gentle with mate in one and slowly climb to mate in three and tricky tactics.

What's inside

  • Level after level of checked, one-solution puzzles: mate in 1, 2 and 3, forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks and promotions
  • Endgame drills: checkmate with queen, rook or two rooks against the engine within a move limit
  • A boss duel against a bot at the end of every chapter
  • Bots: eight original characters from a sleepy owl to the Eclipse Warden, rated about 250 to 2100
  • Choose your colour, take back moves against the first three bots, and pick up an unfinished game later
  • Rated puzzles: an endless ladder matched to your puzzle rating, with streaks and a rating chart
  • Tap or drag to move, legal-move dots, smooth animations and a clear promotion picker
  • A new night every 20 levels: Moonrise Terrace, Rose Garden, Winter Observatory, Lantern Harbor, Desert Palace and Eclipse
  • Soft marble clicks, a chime on check and gentle generative music

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
