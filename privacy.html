Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026. Applies to all Kitefold Games apps (the "games") on iOS and Android.

Who we are

The games are published by Kitefold Games ("we"). Contact: privacy@kitefoldgames.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The games show ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers to show and measure ads, and personalised ads only with your permission where the law requires it.
  • If Firebase Analytics is enabled in a build, it records anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 completed") so we can make the games better.
  • The optional "No Ads" purchase is handled by Apple or Google; RevenueCat checks that the purchase is valid.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, sound/music/vibration settings, language and whether you own "No Ads" are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Consent and your choices

  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time via Settings › Privacy choices in the game.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Google › Ads).
  • Buying "No Ads" removes interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR / KVKK)

Consent for personalised advertising and tracking; our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the games (non-personalised ads, anonymous analytics); performance of a contract for purchases.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and transfers

We do not keep a database of players. Our providers keep data according to their policies (for example, Firebase Analytics keeps event data for 2 to 14 months, depending on the retention setting). Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. Because the games don't identify you, the quickest way to delete ad and analytics identifiers is to reset your advertising ID and delete the app; you can also email us and we will help with requests to our providers. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

We will update this page when the games or the services they use change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
