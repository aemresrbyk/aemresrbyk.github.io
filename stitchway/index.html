Stitchway

Untangle threads, reveal art

Google PlayGalaxy StoreXiaomi GetApps

Drag the pins. Untangle the threads. Watch the embroidery appear.

Stitchway is a calm untangle puzzle set in a wooden embroidery hoop. Colored threads run between brass pins, and wherever two threads cross they pull tight and glow. Slide the pins around the linen until no two threads cross. Then the magic happens: every puzzle is secretly an embroidery motif. The pins glide home, the basting threads pull out and the shapes fill with satin stitches, revealing a finished flower, bird, cat, fish, cottage, snowflake, mandala and more.

What's inside

  • Simple drag controls, a live crossing counter and gentle hints
  • Endless levels that grow from a few pins to big, knotty boss hoops
  • New rules along the way: nailed pins, threads that only tangle with their own color, and stretchy wool yarn
  • Six fabrics, each with its own thread palette and stitch style: Linen Garden, Indigo Sashiko, Folk Red, Pastel Nursery, Autumn Wool and Midnight Velvet
  • A new embroidery for your sampler with every level you finish
  • A daily hoop with a fresh motif every day
  • Soft plucked-string sounds, satisfying stitching zips and up to three stars on every level

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
