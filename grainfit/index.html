Grainfit

Rotate, fit & clear the board

Google PlayGalaxy StoreXiaomi GetApps

A block puzzle with a twist: tap any piece to rotate it.

Grainfit brings the classic fill-the-line puzzle to a warm wooden tabletop. Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board, complete a row or a column to clear it, and clear every gold tile to win the level. Think a move ahead: the level ends when no piece fits.

What's inside

  • Tap to rotate, drag to place: simple to learn, deeper than it looks
  • Goal-based levels: clear all the gold tiles to win
  • Bomb tiles that blast their area and ice tiles that need two clears
  • Combos for clearing several lines at once
  • A new wood and table felt every 20 levels: Oak, Walnut, Cherry, Maple, Ebony and Birch
  • A boss level at the end of every chapter
  • Wooden clacks, kalimba chimes and up to three stars on every level

Fair by design

  • No ads at all in the first 25 levels
  • After that, an occasional ad between levels, never during play
  • Watching a video to continue or to skip a hard level is always optional
  • One optional purchase removes the between-level ads for good
  • No currencies, no loot boxes, no subscriptions

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, no account needed
  • Available in 14 languages
